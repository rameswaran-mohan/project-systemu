# v0.10.24 — one-command start, persona onboarding, one story on every surface

## Added

- **`systemu start`** — the whole first run in one command: walks you through
  provider setup if needed, starts the daemon, waits until the dashboard is
  genuinely accepting connections, then opens your browser. `--no-browser`
  and `--port` for scripts and remote boxes. The individual commands
  (`systemu setup`, `systemu daemon start` / `stop` / `status`) are unchanged.
- **Persona-adaptive onboarding.** The welcome wizard's "How will you use
  Systemu?" answer now shapes starter tasks, the guided tour's route order,
  and each page's first-visit hints — personal, freelance, solo business,
  small team, or enterprise. Switch any time in Settings; every capability
  stays available whichever you pick.
- A fourth starter invites you to ask for something Systemu cannot do yet
  and watch it offer to build the missing tool.
- **Home growth card** — the tools it can use, the workflows and skills it
  has learned, plus a getting-started list that retires itself — and a
  quiet one-at-a-time suggestion card ("Systemu noticed"); declining is one
  click and permanent.

## Changed

- Provider readiness now reads identically on every surface — boot, welcome
  wizard, Settings, doctor. A local Ollama that actually answers counts as
  usable everywhere, and the wizard warns honestly when your model tiers
  route to a provider this machine cannot serve yet, naming the remedy.
- The wizard's Re-check button re-evaluates every provider named in `.env`,
  not just OpenRouter.
- Every dashboard timestamp renders in your local time through one shared
  formatter (Home rail, live events, workflow cards, scroll journals).
- The one-time tool-review card opens in plain English before the detailed
  consent record (which is unchanged).

## Fixed

- The daemon pins its operating vault to your working folder in every
  process it spawns, and refuses an ambiguous location outright instead of
  guessing — the CLI and the daemon can no longer disagree about where your
  data lives.
